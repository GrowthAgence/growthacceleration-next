## Concept: AI Solutions Lab

### Core Idea
5 teams. Same challenges. 5 different approaches. At the end, Europ Assistance collects the best of each team to build their internal AI stack.

Same challenges for all teams because:
- Compare apples to apples — jury can truly evaluate
- Each team finds different angles — EA gets 5 solutions per problem
- Real competition (not artificial)
- Best-of fusion at the end = a solution that no single team could have built alone

### Not just training — a business deliverable
EA doesn't just pay for training. They leave with a concrete AI action plan, tested by their own teams.

## 4 Challenge Ideas (to co-build with Matteo)

### 1. "Claims Accelerator"
Automate triage and summarization of insurance claims with AI.
- Input: 20 fictional (but realistic) claim emails
- Output: classification, priority, draft response

### 2. "Customer Intelligence"
Analyze 6 months of customer feedback (NPS, complaints, reviews) and produce an actionable report with recommendations.
- Which tool, which prompt, which output format

### 3. "Meeting Killer"
Transform the meeting workflow: auto note-taking, structured minutes, extracted action items, automated follow-up.
- Live demo with a fake 10-min meeting

### 4. "Internal Knowledge Bot"
Build an AI assistant that answers internal questions (process, HR, IT, products) from provided documents.
- The best-working bot wins
